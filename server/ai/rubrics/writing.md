# IELTS Writing public band anchors (condensed)

Use these descriptors. Cite student evidence for each criterion. Do not invent chart data.

## Task 1 — Task Achievement
- 5: partially addresses; limited/no clear overview; key features missing or inaccurate
- 6: addresses requirements; overview present; main features selected but may be mechanical; some inaccuracy
- 7: covers requirements; clear overview; key features clearly presented; occasional under/over-generalisation
- 8: covers all requirements sufficiently; well-selected features; accurate detail
- 9: fully satisfies; insightful overview; features fully extended

## Task 2 — Task Response
- 5: partially addresses; unclear/repetitive position; limited ideas
- 6: addresses all parts unevenly; relevant position; ideas relevant but unevenly developed
- 7: addresses all parts; clear position; ideas extended/supported with occasional over-generalisation
- 8: sufficiently addresses all parts; well-developed, well-supported ideas
- 9: fully addresses; fully developed, fully extended ideas

## Coherence & Cohesion
- 5: inadequate organisation; faulty/repetitive cohesion; weak paragraphing
- 6: coherent overall; progression clear; cohesion sometimes mechanical; referencing unclear at times
- 7: logical organisation; clear progression; good cohesive range; clear topic per paragraph
- 8: skilful sequencing; managed cohesion; appropriate paragraphing
- 9: subtle cohesion; skilful paragraphing

## Lexical Resource
- 5: limited range; noticeable word-choice errors; repetitive
- 6: adequate range; less-common attempts with some inaccuracy; errors rarely impede
- 7: flexible range; awareness of style/collocation; occasional errors
- 8: wide range; skilful uncommon items; rare slips
- 9: precise, natural, sophisticated control

## Grammatical Range & Accuracy
- 5: limited range; frequent errors; complex forms usually faulty
- 6: mix of simple/complex; errors rarely impede
- 7: variety of complex structures; frequent error-free sentences
- 8: wide range; majority error-free; rare slips
- 9: full flexibility and accuracy

## Scoring rules
- Each criterion 0–9 in 0.5 steps; overall = mean of four, nearest 0.5
- Under-length (<150 T1 / <250 T2) must lower TA/TR
- Off-topic or memorised material: lower TA/TR sharply
- Every criteriaNotes field must quote or paraphrase concrete student evidence

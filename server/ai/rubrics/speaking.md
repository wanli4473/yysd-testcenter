# IELTS Speaking public band anchors (condensed)

Grade Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy, Pronunciation separately.
STT typos are not pronunciation errors. When audio is provided, judge Pronunciation from audio.

## Fluency & Coherence
- 5: usually maintains flow but needs repetition/self-correction; overuse of certain connectives; simple speech
- 6: willing to speak at length despite hesitation; uses a range of connectives; coherence sometimes lost by repetition
- 7: speaks at length without noticeable effort; some hesitation for language; uses a range of connectives flexibly
- 8: fluent with only occasional repetition; coherent topic development; appropriate cohesive features
- 9: fully fluent; coherent and appropriately developed; rare hesitation only for content

## Lexical Resource
- 5: manages to talk about familiar/unfamiliar topics but with limited flexibility; attempts paraphrase with mixed success
- 6: wide enough vocabulary to discuss topics at length; unclear/wrong word choice does not impede; paraphrase usually successful
- 7: flexible vocabulary; some less common / idiomatic items; effective paraphrase; occasional inaccuracy
- 8: wide resource; skilful use of less common and idiomatic vocabulary; rare inaccuracy
- 9: precise and sophisticated throughout; full flexibility

## Grammatical Range & Accuracy
- 5: basic sentence forms with reasonable accuracy; complex structures limited and often faulty
- 6: mix of simple and complex; errors occur but rarely impede; complex forms may lack flexibility
- 7: range of structures; frequent error-free sentences; some mistakes persist
- 8: wide range; majority error-free; occasional inappropriacies or slips
- 9: full range and flexibility; consistent accuracy; rare slips

## Pronunciation
- 5: usually intelligible; mispronunciation of individual words/sounds reduces clarity at times; limited control of features
- 6: can be understood throughout; mispronunciation of words/sounds reduces clarity at times; uses a range of features with mixed control
- 7: easy to understand; L1 accent may affect intelligibility minimally; shows all positive features of band 6 and some of band 8
- 8: easy to understand; L1 accent has minimal effect; wide range of pronunciation features used flexibly
- 9: precise and effortless; full range of features; L1 accent has no effect

## Evidence rules
- Each detailed score must cite concrete evidence (quote transcript fragment and/or audio observation)
- Pronunciation without audio: estimate cautiously and mark comment that PR is estimated from fluency cues
- Invalid/empty answers lower Fluency and overall
